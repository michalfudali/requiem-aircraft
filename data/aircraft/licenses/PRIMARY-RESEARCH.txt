PRIMARY RESEARCH — REFERENCE AIRCRAFT

Individually attributed factual observations transcribed from public manufacturer
specifications, flight manuals and type-certificate data sheets. The research
manifest identifies the publication, section, original unit and applicable
configuration for every imported numerical observation.

Source publications remain subject to their publishers' terms. They are linked,
not redistributed. This compilation does not grant rights to the original
manuals, brochures, photographs or trademarks.

The catalogue is a dated research snapshot. Published estimates and standard
empty weights are not serial-number-specific weighing or operational data.
